Training Programs

A path, not a catalog

Nine courses across three tiers, sequenced so foundational safety and skill come first, role-specific application comes second, and internal capability comes last. Start with Tier 1 — it's built for employees who have never used AI at work.

Tier 1 · Foundations

Every employee, no exceptions

Goal: safe, confident, daily use. No prior AI exposure assumed.

AI-101

AI at Work: What It Is, What It Isn't

Demystifies LLMs in plain language — what tools like Claude actually do, common misconceptions, and where they reliably help vs. where they fail.

90 min · workshop or on-demand
AI-102

Prompting Essentials

The one practical skill that unlocks everything else: how to give an AI tool enough context to get a useful answer on the first try, with a reusable prompt framework.

Half-day workshop
AI-103

Responsible & Compliant AI Use

Data privacy, confidentiality, what never to paste into a public AI tool, and how to spot and check for wrong or fabricated answers.

60 min
AI-104

AI for Everyday Productivity

Hands-on with real daily tasks — drafting and editing email, summarizing long documents and meeting notes, first drafts of reports and slides.

Half-day workshop
Tier 2 · Applied

By role and function

Goal: AI embedded in the specific job, not just general literacy.

AI-201

AI for Data & BI Teams

Using AI alongside dashboards and reporting tools — query drafting, narrative summaries of data, and QA'ing AI output against source data.

Full-day workshop
AI-202

AI for Managers & Team Leads

Delegating to AI, reviewing AI-assisted work, setting team norms, and coaching a team through adoption without triggering fear of replacement.

Half-day workshop
AI-203

AI for Marketing & Communications

Drafting, editing, and repurposing content responsibly, with attention to brand-voice consistency when AI is in the loop.

Half-day workshop
AI-204

AI for HR & People Operations

Job descriptions, policy drafts, and internal communications — with explicit attention to bias and fairness risks.

Half-day workshop
Tier 3 · Multiply

Building internal capability

Goal: your organization needs us less over time, on purpose.

AI-301

AI Champions: Train-the-Trainer

Certifies a small internal cohort to run onboarding and answer day-to-day questions after we leave. See the full program →

2-day certification
AI-302

Executive AI Briefing

A leadership session on where AI actually pays off, adoption risk, and what to greenlight for the rest of the organization.

90 min
