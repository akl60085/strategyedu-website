About

Alex Lewis has done this before — three times.

Founder of StrategyEdu. Dartmouth-trained engineer. 28 years inside the data and BI functions of Fortune 500 companies, and the instructor who trained the employees who used what he built.

"This is the fourth time I've watched this exact moment happen. In 1998 it was MicroStrategy convincing finance departments a report could update itself. In the years after, it was Teradata and Snowflake convincing IT that the cloud could be trusted with the crown jewels. In between, it was Tableau and Power BI convincing analysts they didn't need to wait on IT to build their own dashboards. Every time, the software was never the hard part — it worked. The hard part was always getting a room of smart, busy, skeptical employees to actually change how they worked, starting Monday. That's the job I've done for 28 years, across seven companies and a dozen platform rollouts. AI is the biggest version of that moment I've ever seen, and it's moving faster than any of the others. I'm not pivoting because I've fallen for a new technology — I'm pivoting because this is exactly the problem I already know how to solve."
— Alex Lewis, Founder

Career

From MicroStrategy's own consulting floor to founding StrategyEdu

A condensed timeline — the full run includes stops at Bank of America, GlaxoSmithKline, LexisNexis, and more, each one an enterprise team learning to trust a new reporting platform.

1998 — Tysons Corner, VA

Joined MicroStrategy, Inc. directly

Started as a Senior Production Consultant implementing the software at the company that built it — interviewing end-users, documenting requirements, building the first reports.

2000 — Los Angeles, CA

Consulting Practice Manager

Promoted to run MicroStrategy's LA consulting practice: hired and managed a team of 20 consultants and owned a $5M annual P&L.

2002

Founded StrategyEdu

Left to build an independent training practice, bringing instructor-led, hands-on MicroStrategy training directly to enterprise teams — including American Express, NBC Universal, Hallmark Cards, and Yahoo.

2003 – 2021

Senior data architect & consultant, Fortune 500 teams

Built reporting systems and delivered instructor-led training inside Lowe's (250+ employees, 20+ e-learning modules), Reynolds American (500+ management reports), GlaxoSmithKline, Bank of America, LexisNexis, and Charter Communications.

2022 — Charlotte, NC

Senior Manager, Hayward Holdings

Built a data analytics team from scratch, stood up SOX-compliant reporting governance, and personally delivered instructor-led Tableau & Power BI training to analysts across the US, EU, and Australia.

2023 — Present

Data Architect Consultant, StrategyEdu

Leading a small data engineering team on a modern BigQuery and Tableau Cloud reporting build — staying current on the tools while continuing to teach.

2026

StrategyEdu relaunches for AI

Same instructor-led training model, same enterprise rigor — now applied to helping corporate teams adopt AI tools like Claude with confidence.
