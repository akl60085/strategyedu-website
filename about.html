About Alex Lewis

I’m Alex Lewis, founder of StrategyEdu and a data analytics professional with more than 25 years of experience helping organizations transform data into useful information for better decision-making.

My career in data and analytics began in 1998 with MicroStrategy, where I implemented enterprise Business Intelligence applications and later managed a consulting practice and a team of software consultants. Since then, I have designed and delivered data and analytics solutions for organizations across financial services, healthcare, retail, telecommunications, manufacturing, and other industries. My work has ranged from gathering business requirements and designing enterprise data models to developing dashboards, reporting applications, data pipelines, and analytics platforms using technologies such as MicroStrategy, Tableau, Power BI, Snowflake, Teradata, BigQuery, Python, and SQL.

Recently, my work has moved upstream from traditional Business Intelligence into data engineering — preparing, organizing, modeling, and governing the data that ultimately powers analytics applications. More recently, I have extended that experience into AI engineering, with a particular focus on preparing enterprise information for use with Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and other generative AI applications.

From Business Intelligence to Artificial Intelligence

I see AI as a natural evolution of the work organizations have been doing with data analytics for decades.

A BI dashboard and a RAG-based AI assistant may look very different to an end user, but they share an important requirement: both are only as reliable as the underlying data architecture.

Traditional analytics requires organizations to identify authoritative data sources, clean and transform data, establish meaningful business definitions, create appropriate data models, and deliver information in a form users can understand and trust. AI engineering introduces new techniques — embeddings, vector databases, semantic search, document chunking, retrieval pipelines, prompt engineering, and LLMs — but many of the underlying data challenges remain remarkably familiar.

This becomes especially important with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). A RAG system connects an LLM with an organization’s own information so that the model can retrieve relevant content when answering a question. Building an effective RAG solution therefore isn’t simply a matter of connecting documents to an AI model. The information must be identified, prepared, structured, segmented, enriched with useful metadata, indexed, retrieved, and evaluated appropriately.

That is where decades of data analytics and data engineering experience become particularly valuable. My background in dimensional and relational data modeling, data quality, reporting governance, business requirements, SQL, database platforms, and enterprise analytics provides a foundation for understanding how organizations can move from raw data, to trusted information, to analytics, and ultimately to AI-enabled knowledge.

AI Training Built Around Real Business Problems

StrategyEdu focuses on instructor-led AI training for mid-market companies and small businesses, particularly organizations in the Southeast looking for practical ways to introduce generative AI into their existing operations.

My approach to AI education is grounded in implementation rather than hype. The objective isn’t simply to teach people how to use a chatbot. It is to help business and technical professionals understand what modern AI can do, where its limitations are, how enterprise data fits into the picture, and how technologies such as LLMs and RAG can be applied responsibly to real organizational problems.

Teaching has also been a recurring part of my career. I have developed interactive web-based training, delivered instructor-led reporting and analytics courses to corporate users, trained more than 250 employees at Lowe’s, and provided Tableau and Power BI visualization training to analysts in the United States, European Union, and Australia. I currently also work in education as an academic tutor.

That combination of analytics consulting, data engineering, AI engineering, and teaching experience shapes the training I provide through StrategyEdu: technically grounded, business-focused, and designed to make sophisticated technology understandable to the people who will actually use it.
