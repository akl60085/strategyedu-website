FAQ

The two questions everyone asks, plus the rest

Straight answers, in plain language — the same tone we use in a workshop.

Is our company data safe if employees use AI tools?

It depends entirely on which tool and which settings — that's exactly what the Responsible & Secure AI Use course (AI-103) exists to cover. We teach employees precisely what should never be pasted into a public AI tool, how to check a vendor's data-handling terms, and what your IT and legal teams should be reviewing before rollout. We're trainers, not your compliance department — but we'll make sure your employees aren't your biggest data risk out of sheer unfamiliarity.

Will this training make employees worry AI is replacing their jobs?

Handled badly, yes. That's why AI-202 (AI for Managers & Business Leaders) exists as its own course, and why every workshop is framed around AI doing specific tasks faster — not replacing the employee's judgment. In practice, the sessions that get the best reception are the ones that are direct about the anxiety instead of pretending it isn't in the room.

Which AI tools do you teach — Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot?

The Foundations curriculum is tool-agnostic on purpose — prompting and judgment skills transfer across tools. Hands-on sessions typically use Claude, and workshops can be tailored to whichever tool your organization has standardized on.

How is this different from a generic AI training vendor?

Most AI training vendors are new companies teaching a new subject. StrategyEdu has been running enterprise instructor-led training since 2002 — the AI curriculum is new, but the training model, the on-site customization, and the instructor delivering it aren't. See the About page for the full background.

How much does training cost?

Pricing is scoped to team size, tier, and delivery format rather than a flat per-seat rate. Book a consultation and we'll put together a proposal for your specific rollout.

Do you offer on-demand or self-paced options, or only live workshops?

Most Tier 1 Foundations content works either as a live workshop or an on-demand session for teams that need to roll it out asynchronously. Tiers 2 and 3 are built as hands-on, instructor-led sessions.

How big or small can an engagement be?

Anywhere from a single team workshop to a multi-hundred-employee rollout with a certified internal AI Champions cohort — the curriculum and pricing scale with it.
