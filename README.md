# StrategyEdu Website

Relaunch of [strategyedu.com](https://www.strategyedu.com) — repositioning from 20+ years of enterprise MicroStrategy (BI) training to applied AI training for corporate employees.

## Project brief

Full positioning, curriculum, and sitemap outline: see `docs/repositioning-brief.md`.

**Summary:**
- **Positioning:** an experienced enterprise software trainer (not an "AI influencer") teaching corporate teams to safely and confidently use AI tools like Claude day to day.
- **Curriculum:** a 3-tier learning path — Foundations (AI-101–104), Applied/role-based (AI-201–204), and Multiply/train-the-trainer (AI-301–302).
- **Sitemap:** Home, About, Training Programs, For Organizations, AI Champions Program, Results, Resources, FAQ, Contact.
