Tier 3 · AI-301

AI Champions: Train-the-Trainer

A two-day certification that turns a small internal cohort into your organization's own AI support system — so adoption keeps going long after the engagement ends. It mirrors the "certified instructor" model StrategyEdu built its reputation on during its MicroStrategy years.

Certify Your Team
Why this exists

Most AI training ends the day the trainer leaves

A single workshop builds a moment of enthusiasm. It doesn't build the habit of a coworker three desks away who can answer "how do I get Claude to actually help with this" on a random Tuesday. That's a different job — and it's the one this program is built to hand off.

Who it's for

3–8 employees per cohort — usually a mix of team leads, power users, and anyone already the "go-to" person for questions in their department.

What they leave with

A certification, a facilitation playbook, and the same core curriculum used in the Foundations tier — ready to teach it themselves.

What your organization gets

A distributed support layer for AI adoption questions, without booking an outside consultant every time someone gets stuck.

Curriculum

Two days, three parts

Day 1 AM — Master the material

Champions go through the full Tier 1 Foundations curriculum themselves, at a deeper level than a standard attendee — including the "why" behind each teaching point.

Day 1 PM — Facilitation skills

How to run a workshop, handle skepticism and pushback, and answer questions from employees who are anxious about AI and their job.

Day 2 — Practice teaching

Each Champion delivers a portion of the curriculum to the group and gets direct feedback before certifying.

Ongoing — Office hours

A standing check-in for the first 90 days so newly certified Champions have support while they build confidence running sessions solo.

Build a team that doesn't need us anymore

Usually paired with a Tier 1 rollout for the rest of the organization — ask how the two fit together.
