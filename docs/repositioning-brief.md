# StrategyEdu — AI Training Repositioning Brief

Prepared August 7, 2026. Full interactive version: see the artifact shared in the planning conversation.

## 1. Starting point

What the current MicroStrategy-era site already gives us:

- **Tenure** — founded 2002, 20+ years running enterprise software training.
- **Client roster** — American Express, NBC Universal, Hallmark Cards, Yahoo.
- **Delivery model** — on-site, customized to the client's own environment and screenshots.
- **Gap** — nothing on the current site signals AI expertise yet; that's the job of the relaunch.

## 2. Positioning

**Thesis:** 20 years training enterprise employees on complex software is the same job AI training is, on a faster cycle. The credibility isn't "I know AI," it's "I know how to train a workforce."

**Tagline options:**
- A — "AI training for people who train enterprise software for a living." (best for continuity/SEO if keeping the StrategyEdu name)
- B — "Turning AI curiosity into everyday capability." (homepage hero / outcome-first)
- C — "From dashboards to Claude: 20 years training the workforces that run on data." (About page narrative)

## 3. Course curriculum

### Tier 1 — Foundations (every employee)
- **AI-101** — AI at Work: What It Is, What It Isn't (90 min)
- **AI-102** — Prompting Essentials (half-day)
- **AI-103** — Responsible & Compliant AI Use (60 min)
- **AI-104** — AI for Everyday Productivity (half-day)

### Tier 2 — Applied (by role)
- **AI-201** — AI for Data & BI Teams (full-day)
- **AI-202** — AI for Managers & Team Leads (half-day)
- **AI-203** — AI for Marketing & Communications (half-day)
- **AI-204** — AI for HR & People Operations (half-day)

### Tier 3 — Multiply (internal capability)
- **AI-301** — AI Champions: Train-the-Trainer (2-day certification)
- **AI-302** — Executive AI Briefing (90 min)

Build order: AI-101, AI-102, AI-103 first — these are what a brand-new corporate buyer asks for.

## 5. Tone & content guidelines

**Lead with:** concrete outcomes, plain-language explanations, instructor credentials, direct answers to privacy/job-security concerns.

**Avoid:** hype language ("revolutionary," "game-changing"), tool-war framing on the homepage, BI jargon outside the BI-specific course, stock robot/circuit-board imagery.

## 6. Build order

1. Write the About page bridge story first.
2. Build AI-101, AI-102, AI-103 as real, deliverable workshops.
3. Launch Home, About, Training Programs, For Organizations, Contact.
4. Add Results and Resources once the first client engagements close.
